# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This document defines expected conduct for human contributors,
maintainers, reviewers, and other participants in this repository.

It does not define authorization, operational behavior, or governance
requirements for AI systems or autonomous agents. Those requirements
are governed separately by the repository's AI governance,
authorization, security, and agent policy documents.

## Expected Conduct

Participants must:

- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and retaliation;
- protect confidential, personal, and security-sensitive information;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- follow repository security and contribution policies;
- respect documented authority, review, and approval boundaries.

## Unacceptable Conduct

Unacceptable conduct includes:

- harassment or discriminatory behavior;
- deliberate misrepresentation;
- unauthorized disclosure of protected information;
- bypassing security, review, or approval requirements;
- using project resources for unlawful or harmful activity.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may review reported conduct and take
proportionate action, including content removal, access restriction,
or removal from the project.

Security vulnerabilities must be reported according to SECURITY.md
and must not be disclosed through public conduct reports.
