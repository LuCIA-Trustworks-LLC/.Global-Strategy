# Code of Conduct

We work with respect, accuracy, fairness, privacy, and human accountability.

Contributors and automated workers must:

- communicate without harassment, discrimination, threats, or deception;
- distinguish facts, assumptions, recommendations, and decisions;
- minimize personal and confidential data;
- report conflicts of interest and significant safety or security concerns;
- preserve dissent and review records on consequential decisions;
- avoid manipulating people through concealed automation or fabricated identity.

Project maintainers may restrict participation to protect people, systems, evidence, and lawful operation. Security reports must follow `SECURITY.md`, not public issues.
